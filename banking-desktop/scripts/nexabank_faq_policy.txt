# NexaBank Complete Policy & Information Guide

## 1. ACCOUNT OPENING POLICY

**Q: How do I open a new account?**
To open a new account at NexaBank, you need to provide the following information:
- Full name of the account holder
- Date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY format)
- National ID (NID) or Passport number
- Phone number
- Complete address
- Occupation
- Initial deposit amount (optional, minimum $0)
- Preferred currency (BDT, USD, EUR, GBP)

**Q: What types of accounts does NexaBank offer?**
NexaBank offers three types of accounts:
1. Checking Account - For everyday transactions, bill payments, and withdrawals
2. Savings Account - For saving money with interest earnings
3. Business Account - For business transactions with higher limits

**Q: Is there a minimum balance requirement?**
- Checking Account: No minimum balance required
- Savings Account: Minimum balance of $100 recommended to earn interest
- Business Account: Minimum balance of $500 recommended

**Q: How long does it take to open an account?**
Account opening is instant through NexaBank's digital platform. Your account number will be generated immediately after submitting all required information.

**Q: Can I open multiple accounts?**
Yes, a single user can open multiple accounts of different types. There is no limit on the number of accounts per user.

**Q: What documents are required to open an account?**
Required documents:
- Valid National ID (NID) or Passport
- Recent photograph
- Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement)
- Phone number for OTP verification

## 2. DEPOSIT POLICY

**Q: What is the minimum deposit amount?**
There is no minimum deposit limit at NexaBank. You can deposit any amount starting from $1.

**Q: What is the maximum single deposit amount?**
The maximum single deposit amount is $50,000. For deposits above this limit, please contact customer support.

**Q: How quickly are deposits reflected in my account?**
All deposits are reflected immediately in real-time. There is no waiting period for deposit transactions.

**Q: Are there any deposit fees?**
NexaBank does not charge any fees for deposits. All deposit transactions are completely free.

**Q: What currencies can I deposit in?**
NexaBank supports deposits in BDT (Bangladeshi Taka), USD (US Dollar), EUR (Euro), and GBP (British Pound).

**Q: Can I schedule a recurring deposit?**
Yes, you can set up automatic recurring deposits on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis through the AI Mode feature.

## 3. WITHDRAWAL POLICY

**Q: What is the daily withdrawal limit?**
Daily withdrawal limits by account type:
- Checking Account: $5,000 per day
- Savings Account: $3,000 per day
- Business Account: $10,000 per day
Limits reset at midnight (12:00 AM) every day.

**Q: What is the minimum withdrawal amount?**
The minimum withdrawal amount is $1 for all account types.

**Q: Are there withdrawal fees?**
NexaBank does not charge fees for standard withdrawals. However, express withdrawals above $10,000 may incur a processing fee of 0.1%.

**Q: Can I withdraw more than my daily limit?**
To request a limit increase, please contact NexaBank customer support with a valid reason. Temporary limit increases may be approved for business purposes.

**Q: What happens if I try to withdraw more than my balance?**
NexaBank does not allow overdrafts. If you try to withdraw more than your available balance, the transaction will be declined automatically.

## 4. PIN & SECURITY POLICY

**Q: How do I set my account PIN?**
You can set your PIN from the Deposit screen by clicking the Set/Change PIN button. Your PIN must be 4 to 6 digits long.

**Q: What happens if I enter the wrong PIN?**
After 3 failed PIN attempts, your account will be temporarily locked for 15 minutes. This is a security measure to protect your account from unauthorized access.

**Q: How do I reset my PIN?**
To reset your PIN, use the Set/Change PIN button on the Deposit screen. You will need to enter your new 4-6 digit PIN.

**Q: Is my PIN stored securely?**
Yes. NexaBank uses industry-standard bcrypt hashing to store PINs. Your actual PIN is never stored in our database only a secure hash.

**Q: What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access?**
Immediately contact NexaBank customer support and change your PIN. Do not share your PIN with anyone, including NexaBank staff.

**Q: How often should I change my PIN?**
NexaBank recommends changing your PIN every 90 days for maximum security. You will receive a notification reminder every 3 months.

## 5. INTEREST RATES

**Q: What is the interest rate for savings accounts?**
Savings accounts earn 4.5% annual interest, calculated monthly and credited to your account on the last day of each month.

**Q: What is the interest rate for checking accounts?**
Checking accounts earn 1.2% annual interest, calculated and credited quarterly.

**Q: What is the interest rate for business accounts?**
Business accounts earn 3.8% annual interest, calculated monthly.

**Q: How is interest calculated?**
Interest is calculated using the daily average balance method:
Interest = (Average Daily Balance x Annual Rate x Number of Days) / 365

**Q: Is interest taxable?**
Yes, interest earned on NexaBank accounts is subject to applicable taxes as per government regulations. NexaBank will provide an annual interest statement for tax purposes.

## 6. TRANSFER POLICY

**Q: Are internal transfers free?**
Yes. Transfers between NexaBank accounts are completely free with no transaction fees.

**Q: What are the fees for external transfers?**
External bank transfers incur a fee of 0.5% of the transfer amount, with a minimum fee of $2 and a maximum fee of $25.

**Q: What is the transfer limit?**
- Internal transfers: Up to $25,000 per transaction
- External transfers: Up to $10,000 per transaction per day

**Q: How long do external transfers take?**
External transfers typically take 1-3 business days depending on the receiving bank.

**Q: Can I cancel a transfer?**
Internal transfers are instant and cannot be cancelled. External transfers can be cancelled within 1 hour of initiation by contacting customer support.

## 10. KYC POLICY

**Q: What is KYC and why is it required?**
KYC (Know Your Customer) is a mandatory process to verify the identity of account holders. It helps prevent fraud, money laundering, and unauthorized account usage.

**Q: What documents are needed for KYC?**
For individual accounts: National ID or Passport, recent photograph, proof of address, phone number.
For business accounts: Trade license, company registration documents, owner NID or Passport, business address proof.

**Q: How long does KYC verification take?**
KYC verification is completed within 24-48 hours of document submission.

**Q: What happens if KYC is not completed?**
Accounts without completed KYC will have restricted transaction limits:
- Maximum deposit: $500
- Maximum withdrawal: $200
- No external transfers allowed

## 13. ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY

Contact NexaBank customer support. You must withdraw all funds before closure. Account closure takes 3-7 business days.

**Q: Is there a fee for closing an account?**
No fee for closing a standard checking or savings account. Business accounts may have a closing fee of $25 if closed within 6 months of opening.

**Q: What happens to my remaining balance when I close?**
Your remaining balance will be transferred to another NexaBank account or issued as a bank check within 5 business days.

Closed accounts cannot be reopened. You will need to open a new account.

## 16. DORMANT ACCOUNT POLICY

**Q: When does an account become dormant?**
An account becomes dormant if there are no transactions for 12 consecutive months.

**Q: What happens to a dormant account?**
No interest is earned, a monthly maintenance fee of $2 may apply, and account access is restricted to balance inquiries only.

**Q: How do I reactivate a dormant account?**
Contact NexaBank customer support with valid ID proof. A reactivation fee of $10 applies. The account will be reactivated within 24 hours.
